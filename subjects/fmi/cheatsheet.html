Financial Markets & Instruments — Cheat Sheet

Every formula, ratio and decision rule from the course on one page: accounting → statements → ratios → costing → TVM → capital budgeting → WACC → valuation → CAPM & portfolios → derivatives.

← Full sessions

S1 Financial accounting basics

  • Accounting = record, classify, summarise (in money) and interpret transactions. Users: internal (management, employees) vs external (investors, lenders, suppliers, government).
  • Equation: Assets = Liabilities + Equity; Equity = A − L. Expanded: A = L + Capital + Income − Expenses + Gains − Losses − Drawings.
  • Equity: proprietor = Capital + Profit − Drawings; company = Share capital + Retained earnings + Reserves (dividends cut RE, not share capital).
  • Gain/(loss) on sale = Proceeds − Carrying amount (Cost − Accumulated depreciation).
  • 9 concepts: entity, going concern, money measurement, accounting period, historical cost, dual aspect, accrual, matching, realisation. 4 conventions: consistency, prudence, full disclosure, materiality.
  • Matching: COGS = units sold × cost (unsold = inventory). Revenue recognition: upfront ₹12,000 for 12 months → ₹1,000/month; rest is a liability.
  • Check every transaction: ΔA = ΔL + ΔE. Credit sale → receivable ↑ + equity ↑ (by sale − COGS).
  • Trap: drawings/dividends and customer advances are not expenses/income; profit from capital = Closing − Opening − Additional capital + Drawings.

S2 Financial statements

  • Balance sheet (a date): non-current + current assets = equity + non-current + current liabilities. P&L / CFS: a period.
  • P&L: Revenue − COGS = GP − opex (excl. D&A) = EBITDA − D&A = EBIT + other income − finance costs = PBT − tax = PAT. EPS = PAT / shares.
  • COGS = Opening inventory + Purchases − Closing inventory.
  • Links: Closing RE = Opening RE + PAT − Dividends; Opening cash + CFO + CFI + CFF = Closing cash.
  • Capex vs revenue exp.: benefit > 1 year → capitalise + depreciate (CFI); used up now → expense (CFO).

S2 Depreciation & cash flow

  • SLM = (Cost − Residual) / Life (=SLN(cost,salvage,life)). WDV = Opening carrying amount × rate; rate to hit residual r = 1 − (S/C)1/n.
  • Part year: annual × months/12. Carrying amount = Cost − Accumulated depreciation.
  • CFO (indirect, slides): PBT + D&A + finance costs − interest/dividend income − gains + losses ± ΔWC − tax paid.
  • ΔWC: current asset ↑ → subtract; current liability ↑ → add. Tax paid = opening payable + expense − closing payable.
  • Proforma classes: interest/dividend received → investing; interest/dividend paid, loans, shares, buyback → financing.
  • Trap: WDV on original cost is SLM; subtract gains (proceeds go in CFI); profit ≠ cash.

S3 Ratio analysis I — profitability & leverage

  • Five families: liquidity · efficiency · solvency/leverage · profitability & return · market/investor. Compare over time, vs peers, vs industry, vs budget.
  • GPM = (Revenue − Cost of sales)/Revenue × 100 · EBITDA margin = EBITDA/Revenue × 100.
  • OPM = EBIT/Revenue × 100 · NPM = PAT/Revenue × 100 — every margin divides by revenue.
  • D/E = Total debt/Shareholders' equity; debt = interest-bearing debt (or total liabilities — say which); equity = share capital + reserves & surplus; < 1 usually comfortable.
  • ICR = EBIT/Interest expense; > 3x comfortable, < 1x means EBIT can't cover interest.
  • Also used: debt ratio = debt/TA · equity multiplier = TA/equity · DSCR = (PAT + Dep + Interest)/(Interest + Principal), > 1.25–1.5x.
  • Leverage lifts ROE when EBIT/assets > interest rate and deepens losses when EBIT falls (interest is fixed).
  • Traps: margin (÷ price) ≠ markup (÷ cost); NPM uses PAT not PBT; trade payables are liabilities, not debt.

S4 Liquidity, efficiency & operating cycle

  • Working capital: gross = current assets; net = CA − CL.
  • Current = CA/CL (~2 : 1) · Quick = (CA − Inventory)/CL (~1 : 1) · Cash = (Cash + equivalents + marketable securities)/CL.
  • Inventory TO = COGS/Avg inventory; holding period = Avg inventory/COGS × 365.
  • Receivables TO = Net credit sales/Avg receivables; collection period = Avg receivables/Net credit sales × 365.
  • Payables TO = Credit purchases (or COGS)/Avg payables; payment period = Avg payables/Credit purchases × 365.
  • Operating cycle = Inventory days + Receivable days − Payable days (= cash conversion cycle); negative = suppliers fund you.
  • Traps: average = (opening + closing)/2 (=AVERAGE(B2:C2)); COGS, not sales, for inventory; subtract payable days; paying creditors at year-end lifts CR (window dressing).

S4 Returns, DuPont & market ratios

  • ROCE = EBIT/Capital employed × 100; CE = Total assets − CL = Equity + Long-term debt.
  • ROA = PAT/Avg total assets × 100 · ROE = PAT/Avg shareholders' equity × 100.
  • ROI = (Gain − Investment cost)/Investment cost × 100 — ignores time.
  • DuPont: ROE = NPM × Asset turnover × Equity multiplier = PAT/Rev × Rev/Avg TA × Avg TA/Avg equity.
  • EPS = (PAT − Preference dividend)/No. of ordinary shares · P/E = Market price/EPS.
  • DPS = Ordinary dividends/Shares · Payout = DPS/EPS × 100 · Dividend yield = DPS/Market price × 100.
  • Market cap = Price × Shares outstanding · EV = Market cap + Total debt − Cash & equivalents.
  • Traps: EBIT pairs with capital employed, PAT with equity; high ROE may be borrowed (check EM); yield uses market price, not face value.

S5 Cost concepts & behaviour

  • Management accounting: internal, future-focused information for planning (budgets), control (actual vs budget → variances) and decisions (make/buy, pricing); cost accounting is its core.
  • Cost ≠ Expense: expense = cost charged against this period's revenue (machine ₹12 lakh = cost; ₹2.4 lakh depreciation = expense). Cost per unit = Total cost ÷ Units.
  • Classify one cost 5 ways: nature (material/labour/expenses), function (Total = Production + Admin + Selling + Distribution), traceability (direct vs indirect = overheads), behaviour, relevance.
  • Fixed: constant total, per unit = TFC ÷ units falls · Variable = VC/unit × output, per unit constant · Semi-variable = fixed + rate × activity (₹50,000 + ₹8/MH).
  • High-low: b = (CostH − CostL) ÷ (ActivityH − ActivityL); a = CostH − b·ActivityH. Pick highest/lowest activity, not cost. Regression check: =SLOPE, =INTERCEPT.
  • Traps: direct ≠ variable (lubricants: indirect + variable); mark-up on cost ≠ margin on sales (₹80 at 20% margin = 80 ÷ 0.8 = ₹100).

S5 Cost sheet, absorption & relevant costs

  • Product cost = DM + DL + production overheads · OAR = Budgeted overhead ÷ Budgeted activity; absorbed = OAR × actual activity; absorbed > actual → over-absorbed (add to profit).
  • Cost sheet: DM consumed (opening + purchases + carriage in − closing) + DL + DE = prime → + factory OH ± WIP = works cost → + QC − scrap = cost of production → ± finished stock = COGS → + admin + S&D = cost of sales → + profit = sales.
  • Relevant cost = future + differs between options. Ignore sunk (₹2 crore already spent) and committed costs; include opportunity cost (₹16 lakh salary forgone for an MBA).
  • Marginal cost = ΔTotal cost ÷ ΔOutput (₹60,000 ÷ 100 = ₹600) ≈ VC/unit · Contribution = Sales − VC = SP − MC per unit. Marginal costing ≠ marginal cost.
  • Minimum price: spare capacity → MC; full capacity (demand > capacity) → MC + contribution forgone. Airline: ₹1,000 + ₹6,000 = ₹7,000 → reject ₹3,000 (₹20,000 less contribution).

S6 CVP, break-even & margin of safety

  • Contribution = Sales − VC = (SP − VC) × units · Profit = Total contribution − Total FC · S − V = F + P.
  • P/V ratio = Contribution ÷ Sales × 100 (₹200 ÷ ₹500 = 40%) = ΔProfit ÷ ΔSales × 100 for two periods.
  • BEP units = FC ÷ CM per unit (round up) · BEP ₹ = FC ÷ P/V (₹2,00,000 ÷ 0.40 = ₹5,00,000).
  • MoS = Actual − BEP (units or ₹); MoS % = MoS ÷ Actual sales × 100; Profit = MoS ₹ × P/V.
  • Target volume = (FC + Target profit) ÷ CM ((₹12L + ₹8L) ÷ ₹200 = 10,000 units); after-tax target ÷ (1 − t) first.
  • Absorption vs marginal: absorption puts fixed production OH in stock; profit gap = (Closing − Opening stock units) × fixed OH/unit; production > sales → absorption profit higher.
  • What-if: price/VC changes move CM & P/V; FC moves BEP; volume moves only profit & MoS. Excel: =ROUNDUP(B3/(B1-B2),0), Goal Seek profit = 0.

S6 Marginal-costing decisions

  • Rule: decide on the change in total contribution after avoidable fixed costs and opportunity costs; apportioned fixed costs that stay are irrelevant.
  • Make or buy: make if VC + avoidable FC + opportunity cost of capacity < purchase price.
  • Special order: spare capacity → accept if price > MC + order-specific costs; at capacity → also add contribution lost on displaced sales.
  • Drop a line only if its avoidable FC > its contribution, or the capacity earns more elsewhere · Shut-down point = Avoidable FC ÷ CM.
  • Key factor: rank by CM per unit of the scarce resource (CM ÷ hours per unit), not CM per unit or P/V.
  • Trap: rejecting an order priced below full cost but above marginal cost when capacity is idle.

S7 Time value of money

  • FV = PV(1+r)n · PV = FV/(1+r)n; DF = 1/(1+r)n. =FV(r,n,0,-PV), =PV(r,n,0,FV).
  • m compoundings: FV = PV(1+r/m)mn; EAR = (1+r/m)m − 1 (2%/month: APR 24%, EAR 26.82%). =EFFECT. Rule of 72: doubling ≈ 72/r%.
  • CAGR = (FV/PV)1/n − 1 =RATE; n = ln(FV/PV)/ln(1+r) =NPER.
  • Uneven CFs: PV = Σ CFt/(1+r)t; =NPV(r,CF1:CFn) starts at year 1 — add CF0 outside.
  • Annuity: PV = A[1−(1+r)−n]/r (PVIFA); FV = A[(1+r)n−1]/r (FVIFA); due = ordinary × (1+r), Excel type = 1.
  • EMI = P·r(1+r)n/[(1+r)n−1], monthly r and n; =PMT(r/12,n,-P), =IPMT/=PPMT. Interest = opening × r; principal = EMI − interest.
  • Perpetuity CF/r; growing CF1/(r−g), r > g. TVn = CFn(1+g)/(r−g), then ÷ (1+r)n.
  • Trap: match r to the period (monthly rate with months); "flat" 12% ≈ 21.5% reducing; discount TV by year n, not n+1.

S8 Capital budgeting

  • Cash flows: incremental, after tax; OCF = (R−C)(1−t) + tD. Include opportunity cost, working capital (out, then back), salvage; exclude sunk costs and interest.
  • NPV = Σ CFt/(1+r)t − I0; accept if > 0; mutually exclusive → highest NPV. =NPV(r,CF1:CFn)+CF0.
  • IRR: r where NPV = 0; accept if IRR > cost of capital. Interpolate: L + NPVL/(NPVL−NPVH) × (H−L). =IRR(CF0:CFn), =XIRR.
  • Conflicts (scale, timing/reinvestment, multiple IRRs when signs change twice): NPV wins. Crossover rate = IRR of (A − B).
  • Payback = years before recovery + remaining/next CF (equal flows: I/CF); discounted payback uses CF/(1+r)t, always longer. Both ignore post-payback CFs.
  • PI = PV of inflows / I0; PI > 1 ⇔ NPV > 0. Rationing: rank by PI if divisible; lumpy projects → max total NPV of feasible combos.
  • Bharat AutoParts (₹80 L, ₹25 L × 5, 12%): NPV ₹10.12 L, IRR 16.99%, payback 3.2 yr, discounted 4.29 yr, PI 1.13.
  • Trap: putting CF0 inside =NPV (gives NPV ÷ (1+r)); choosing the higher IRR between mutually exclusive projects.

S9 Cost of equity

  • Cost of capital = minimum return investors require = opportunity cost = hurdle/discount rate.
  • Beta β = Cov(Ri, Rm)/Var(Rm) (both ÷ n − 1) = regression slope; =SLOPE(stock, market). Only systematic risk is priced.
  • CAPM Ke = Rf + β(Rm − Rf); Rf = 10-yr G-Sec. e.g. 7% + 1.2 × 6% = 14.2%.
  • Alpha α = actual − [Rf + β(Rm − Rf)]; > 0 = skill.
  • DGM Ke = D1/P0 + g, D1 = D0(1 + g); dividend payers only, needs g < Ke.
  • g = (Dn/D0)1/n − 1 (n = years between) or b × ROE, b = 1 − DPS/EPS.
  • Trap: β × Rm instead of β × (Rm − Rf); D0 instead of D1.

S9 Cost of debt & WACC

  • Pre-tax Kd = current loan rate, or bond YTM (not coupon); mix → market-value-weighted average.
  • YTM ≈ [C + (F − P)/n] / [(F + P)/2]; exact =RATE(n, C, -P, F).
  • After tax Kd(1 − t): ₹20 cr interest at 25% tax costs only ₹15 cr. Kp = Dp/P0, no tax shield.
  • WACC = Kd(1 − t)·D/V + Kp·P/V + Ke·E/V, V = E + P + D.
  • Weights: target structure at market values (E = price × shares). Market WACC usually > book (Reliance 12.2% vs 11.1%).
  • Use: hurdle rate for average-risk projects; FCFF discount rate. More debt lowers WACC at first, then raises it.
  • Trap: pre-tax Kd in WACC, book weights, or discounting a "loan-funded" project at Kd.

S10 Bonds

  • Debt: contractual, fixed coupon, priority over equity, no vote, finite maturity, tax-deductible interest.
  • Terms: face value F (repaid at redemption), coupon C = coupon rate × F, market price P, YTM.
  • Coupon bond P = ΣC/(1 + r)t + F/(1 + r)T; =-PV(r, T, C, F). Coupon < yield → discount; > → premium.
  • Zero-coupon P = F/(1 + r)T (T-Bills; no reinvestment risk). Perpetual P = C/r (bank AT1s; face never repaid).
  • YTM ≈ [C + (F − P)/n] / [(F + P)/2]; exact =RATE(n, C, -P, F). Discount: coupon < C/P < YTM.
  • Trap: discounting at the coupon rate; price and yield move opposite ways.

S10 Stocks — dividend discount models

  • Zero growth P0 = D1/r (also irredeemable preference Dp/r).
  • Gordon P0 = D1/(r − g), D1 = D0(1 + g); only if r > g. e.g. 19 × 1.10/0.04 = ₹522.50.
  • Two-stage P0 = Σ PV(D1…Dn) + [Dn+1/(r − g2)]/(1 + r)n; =NPV(r, D1:Dn) + Pn/(1+r)^n.
  • Decision: intrinsic value > market price → undervalued (buy).
  • Trap: D0 for D1; terminal price from Dn or discounted n + 1 years.

S11 Free cash flow, FCFF & FCFE

  • OCF = NP + depreciation − ↑inventory − ↑receivables + ↑payables; FCF = OCF − CapEx (class: 1,000 → 1,090 → ₹790 crore).
  • FCFF = EBIT(1 − T) + Dep − CapEx − ΔNWC → all investors; discount at WACC → enterprise value.
  • FCFE = NI + Dep − CapEx − ΔNWC + Net borrowing = FCFF − Interest(1 − T) + Net borrowing → shareholders; discount at Ke.
  • Net borrowing = new debt raised − debt repaid (negative if net repayment).
  • CFO route: FCFF ≈ CFO − CapEx; FCFE = CFO − CapEx − full interest + net borrowing (tax shield already in CFO).
  • Debt changes FCFE, never FCFF. Negative FCFF = heavy reinvestment, not necessarily a bad business.
  • Trap: ↑current asset = cash used (subtract); CapEx is an outflow; never discount FCFE at WACC or FCFF at Ke.

S11 Excel DCF model & multiples

  • Layout: inputs (blue) → calculations (black) → outputs; one formula per row copied across; lock inputs with $B$3 (F4).
  • Revenuet = Revenuet−1 × (1 + g); EBIT = revenue × margin; ΔNWC = NWCt − NWCt−1.
  • TV = FCFFn(1 + g)/(WACC − g), discounted with the year-n factor; needs g < WACC.
  • EV = Σ PV(FCFF) + PV(TV); equity = EV − debt + cash; ÷ shares = value per share.
  • =NPV(r, CF1:CFn)+CF0 (skips t = 0) · =XNPV(r, CF0:CFn, dates) (includes t = 0) · =IRR, =XIRR, =PMT, =SUMPRODUCT, =XLOOKUP, =CHOOSE.
  • Data Table = output grid for WACC × g (input cells on the same sheet); Goal Seek = one input to hit one target (e.g. implied margin at market price).
  • Checks: balance sheet balances, PV sum = NPV(), g < WACC, TV share of EV (often 70–80%).
  • Multiples: EV = market cap + debt − cash. P/E (trailing vs forward), P/B → equity; EV/EBITDA, EV/Sales → EV, then − debt + cash. Peer MEDIAN × target metric ÷ shares; EV multiples ignore leverage.

S12 Efficient markets

  • EMH: prices fully reflect available information → no consistent abnormal returns after risk and costs.
  • Weak: past prices/volumes → technical analysis useless. Semi-strong: all public info → fundamental analysis useless. Strong: all info incl. insider → rejected (SEBI insider-trading rules on UPSI).
  • Nested: strong ⊃ semi-strong ⊃ weak. Random walk: Pt = Pt−1 + μ + εt.
  • Event study: AR = R − E(R), E(R) = α + βRm; CAR = Σ AR. One jump on day 0, flat after = semi-strong efficient.
  • Anomalies: momentum, size, value, January, day-of-week, post-earnings drift, IPO pricing, bubbles.
  • Biases: overconfidence, herding, loss aversion/disposition effect, anchoring, recency.
  • Trap: efficient ≠ always right; one lucky winner doesn't disprove EMH.

S12 Risk, beta & CAPM

  • E(R) = Σ pR; σ2 = Σ p(R − E(R))2; history: s2 divides by n − 1 (=STDEV.S); CV = σ/E(R).
  • Total risk σ2 = β2σm2 + σε2: systematic (β, not diversifiable, rewarded) + unsystematic (diversifiable, not rewarded).
  • β = Cov(Ri, Rm)/Var(Rm) = ρσi/σm = =SLOPE(stock, market); portfolio β = Σ wiβi.
  • CAPM: E(Ri) = Rf + β(E(Rm) − Rf); e.g. 7% + 1.25 × 5% = 13.25% = Ke.
  • SML: forecast > required → above line → undervalued (α > 0, buy); below → overvalued.
  • Fair price P0 = (P1 + D1)/(1 + Ke).
  • Trap: Rf + β × Rm (forgot − Rf); mixing COVARIANCE.S with VAR.P; swapping SLOPE arguments.

S13 Portfolio theory

  • Return E(Rp) = wAE(RA) + wBE(RB) — always a weighted average.
  • Risk σp2 = wA2σA2 + wB2σB2 + 2wAwBCov; Cov = ρσAσB. ρ = +1 → plain weighted avg; ρ = −1 → can reach 0.
  • Min-variance wA* = (σB2 − Cov)/(σA2 + σB2 − 2Cov); mixes above the MVP are efficient, below it are dominated.
  • Diversification removes unsystematic risk only; systematic (market) risk stays → measured by β.
  • Sharpe = (Rp − Rf)/σp; pick the highest. Treynor = (Rp − Rf)/β.
  • CML E(Rp) = Rf + [(Rm − Rf)/σm]σp (σ, efficient portfolios) vs SML uses β (any asset).
  • Excel: =STDEV.S, =COVARIANCE.S, =CORREL. Trap: forgetting the 2 or squaring the weights.

S13 Valuing bonds & shares

  • Bond price = C × PVIFA(r, n) + F/(1 + r)n at the YTM. Semi-annual: C/2, r/2, 2n.
  • Coupon > YTM → premium; = → par; < → discount (coupon < CY < YTM). Yield up → price down; longer duration → bigger swing.
  • CY = coupon/price. YTM ≈ [C + (F − P)/n]/[(F + P)/2]; exact =RATE(n,C,-P,F); =PRICE/=YIELD per ₹100.
  • DDM zero growth P0 = D/Ke; Gordon P0 = D1/(Ke − g), D1 = D0(1 + g), needs Ke > g.
  • Two-stage: PV of explicit dividends + PV of PN = DN+1/(Ke − g2), discounted N years (not N + 1).
  • P/E value = EPS1 × P/E; justified P/E = payout/(Ke − g). Intrinsic > market → buy; < → sell.

S14 Forwards & futures

  • Derivative: value derived from an underlying (stock, index, commodity, currency, rate). Users: hedgers, speculators, arbitrageurs.
  • Forward payoff long = (ST − K) × qty, short = (K − ST) × qty; OTC, customised, counterparty risk.
  • Futures = standardised, exchange-traded, clearing corp (novation), daily MTM = ΔF × lot size; P&L = (exit − entry) × lot.
  • Margin: balance < maintenance → margin call back to initial margin. Leverage ≈ 1/margin % (12% → 8.3×).
  • Hedge N = β × portfolio value/(futures price × lot); sell N lots to protect a long portfolio.

S14 Futures pricing & options intro

  • Cost of carry F = S·erT (or S(1 + r)T); with dividends F = (S − PV(D))·erT; index F = S·e(r − q)T. T in years.
  • Basis = spot − futures → 0 at expiry (convergence). Contango F > S; backwardation F < S.
  • Arbitrage: Fmkt > fair → borrow, buy spot, sell futures (cash-and-carry); < fair → reverse.
  • Options: buyer has the right, seller the obligation. Call = max(ST − K, 0), put = max(K − ST, 0); profit = payoff − premium.
  • Breakeven: call K + premium, put K − premium. Excel: =S*EXP(r*T), =IF(bal<maint,init-bal,0). Trap: basis is spot − futures.

S15 Options & hedging

  • Call = right to buy, put = right to sell at K by T; buyer pays premium, writer takes the obligation. Payoff: call max(ST − K, 0), put max(K − ST, 0); BE call K + c, put K − p.
  • Moneyness: call ITM if S > K, put ITM if S < K. Premium = intrinsic + time value; TV is highest ATM and decays to 0 at expiry.
  • American (exercise any day) ≥ European (expiry only). NSE options are European (CE/PE). Never exercise an American call early on a non-dividend stock — sell it.
  • Premium drivers: S↑ → call↑ put↓; K↑ → call↓ put↑; T↑, σ↑ → both↑; r↑ → call↑ put↓; dividends↑ → call↓ put↑.
  • Put–call parity: C + K·e−rT = P + S0 (European); a gap = arbitrage.
  • Binomial: Δ = (Cu − Cd)/(Su − Sd); p = (1 + r − d)/(u − d); C0 = [pCu + (1 − p)Cd]/(1 + r). Black–Scholes in Excel via =NORM.S.DIST(d,TRUE).
  • Hedging: protective put max loss = (S0 − K) + p, BE S0 + p; index lots = β × V / (index × lot). Futures lock both ways; options insure one way for a premium.
  • Trap: writing a put is bullish; "ITM" doesn't mean profitable — the premium must be covered.

S16 Option payoffs

  • Profit = payoff − premium (buyer) · premium − payoff (writer); per lot = per share × lot size.
  • Long call: max loss c, BE K + c, upside unlimited · short call: max gain c, loss unlimited.
  • Long put: max gain K − p, max loss p, BE K − p · short put: max gain p, max loss K − p.
  • Covered call (+S, −C): max profit (K − S0) + c, BE = max loss = S0 − c; income, upside capped.
  • Covered put (−S, −P): max profit (S0 − K) + p, BE S0 + p, loss unlimited if the stock rises.
  • Protective put (+S, +P): max loss (S0 − K) + p, BE S0 + p · protective call (−S, +C): max loss (K − S0) + c, BE S0 − c. Covered ↔ protective are exact mirrors.
  • Trap: covered put = short stock + short put (income) ≠ protective put = long stock + long put (insurance).

S16 Option strategies

  • Bull call spread +C(K1) −C(K2), K1 < K2: max profit (K2 − K1) − debit, max loss debit, BE K1 + debit.
  • Bear put spread +P(K1) −P(K2), K1 > K2: max profit (K1 − K2) − debit, max loss debit, BE K1 − debit.
  • Long straddle +C(K) +P(K): max loss c + p, BEs K ± (c + p). Long strangle (OTM call + OTM put): cheaper, needs a bigger move.
  • Pick by view: bullish → long call / bull call spread / short put; bearish → long put / bear put spread / short call; big move → straddle / strangle; calm → covered call, short straddle; insure → protective put / call.
  • Unlimited loss: short call, short straddle / strangle, covered put — margin calls, gap risk.
  • Slope rule: +S = +1 everywhere; +C = +1 above K; −C = −1 above K; +P = −1 below K; −P = +1 below K.
